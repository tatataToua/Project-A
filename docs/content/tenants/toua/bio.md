# Bio

## Who I am

I'm Toua Lee, a full-stack product engineer based in Charlotte, NC. I build AI feature
proof-of-concepts fast — validating them against real product data before handing them
off to engineering for production. Right now I'm deliberately deepening my AI/LLM
engineering skills to move from "builds AI POCs" to "AI engineer" full time.

## Background

I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from the University of North Carolina at Charlotte
(Dec 2024). Since then I've worked across product engineering and IT operations roles
that all share a theme: taking a messy, manual process and replacing it with something
automated and reliable.

At Revmatics, I work as a Product Engineer building AI proof-of-concepts on the OpenAI
and Anthropic APIs. One of these — an AI brand audit tool that maps prospect website
content to persona models — was adopted by engineering and shipped as the company's
top-of-funnel lead magnet. I also built an audience-mapping POC using embeddings and
cosine similarity to rank customer personas for ad targeting, and a conversational AI
layer that lets marketers query retail dashboard data in plain English instead of
reading static reports. Outside of AI work, I fixed inventory SQL powering the
Feastables dashboard, eliminating false out-of-stock statuses across 10,000 retail
locations.

Before that, as an IT Operations Intern at YourBookTeam, I consolidated scattered email
and support requests from 150+ remote employees into a single Slack intake channel,
automated intern onboarding with Zapier (cutting setup from a full day to 45 minutes per
hire), and rebuilt account permission tiers around least-privilege access to eliminate
shared-credential access to payment and password data.

I also worked as a server/host at MOA Korean BBQ in Charlotte concurrently with these
roles — it's where I sharpened the kind of fast, high-pressure, detail-under-load
judgment that carries over well into shipping software.

On the side, I built Switch, a full-stack talent agency platform for DJs (Next.js,
Supabase, PostgreSQL) where DJs onboard, manage profiles, and publish sets and
electronic press kits for promoters — with automated availability and booking
coordination that replaced scheduling the agency used to manage entirely over phone and
email.

I want to be an AI engineer — not just someone who calls an LLM API from a product
