# About Two Owls Tavern

## Our story

Two Owls Tavern opened in 2015 in Millbrook, Oregon, founded by siblings Mara and
Theo Whitfield. Mara trained as a line cook in Portland for six years before coming
home; Theo ran the front of house at a downtown wine bar. They took over a
100-year-old former hardware store on Elm Street, kept the exposed brick and the
original tin ceiling, and built a wood-fired oven into what used to be the loading
dock. The name comes from a pair of great horned owls that nested in the building's
eaves during construction and, according to Mara, "supervised the whole renovation."

We're a New American tavern: wood-fired pizzas and roasted vegetables, a rotating
list of entrees built around whatever's in season from the farms within about thirty
miles of town, and a drinks program that leans heavily on Pacific Northwest wine and
beer. Nothing on the menu is trying to be fancy. We just want it to be very good.

## Reservations

Walk-ins are always welcome. Reservations are recommended for parties of 5 or more,
and for any party on a Friday or Saturday night. Book by phone or through the
"Reservations" link on our website; we hold reserved tables for 15 minutes past the
booked time before releasing them.

## Dress code and seating

Casual — come as you are. We have a covered, heated patio that's dog-friendly (water
bowls provided), a 6-seat counter overlooking the wood-fired oven, and a private
back room that seats up to 20 for events. High chairs and booster seats are
available on request.

## Parking

There's a small lot behind the building (12 spaces, unmarked entrance off the alley
on Birch Street) plus metered street parking along Elm. The lot fills up fast on
weekend evenings — street parking or the public garage two blocks north on 3rd
Avenue is usually a safer bet after 6 PM on Fridays and Saturdays.

## House policies

- For parties of 8 or more, an 18% gratuity is added automatically and we can't
  split the check more than 4 ways.
- We accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-sensitive diets — see the FAQ for
  details. Please flag any allergy to your server rather than assuming a dish is
  automatically safe.
- Gift cards are sold in any denomination at the host stand or online, and never
  expire.
