## Do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are always welcome. We recommend a reservation for parties of 5 or more,
and for any party dining on a Friday or Saturday night, since those slots fill up.
Book by phone at (555) 019-2288 or through the Reservations link on our website. We
hold a reserved table for 15 minutes past the booked time before releasing it.

## Where do I park?

There's a small 12-space lot behind the building, entrance off the alley on Birch
Street, plus metered street parking along Elm. The lot tends to fill up on weekend
evenings — after 6 PM on Fridays and Saturdays, street parking or the public garage
two blocks north on 3rd Avenue is usually easier.

## Is there a dress code?

No — Two Owls Tavern is casual. Come as you are.

## Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards are available in any denomination at the host stand or on our
website, and they never expire.

## Can you host private events?

Yes. Our back room seats up to 20 and can be booked for private dinners, rehearsal
dinners, or small work events. Contact us by phone to check availability and discuss
a set menu — we typically ask for two weeks' notice for parties over 12.

## Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes. We regularly accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-sensitive diets — several
menu items are marked accordingly, and a few can be adapted on request (see the
Menu). If you have a food allergy, please tell your server directly rather than
relying on the menu notes alone, since preparation can vary.

## Is the patio dog-friendly?

Yes, our covered, heated patio welcomes dogs, and we keep water bowls on hand.

## Do you split checks?

For parties of 7 or fewer, yes, we're happy to split the check as needed. For
parties of 8 or more, an 18% gratuity is added automatically and the check can be
split at most 4 ways.

## Do you offer catering or takeout?

We offer takeout for most of the menu — call ahead and we'll have it ready at the
host stand. We don't currently offer off-site catering.

## What are your holiday hours?

We're closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. Hours may be
shortened around other holidays; call ahead if you're planning a visit near one.
