## Starters

### Wood-Roasted Marrow Bones — $16
Two beef marrow bones roasted in the wood-fired oven, served with grilled sourdough,
parsley-caper salad, and flaky salt. **Contains**: gluten. Can be made dairy-free on
request.

### Charred Shishito Peppers — $11
Blistered shishito peppers, lemon, and shaved pecorino. **Contains**: dairy.
Vegetarian.

### Two Owls Chowder — $13
Clam and smoked bacon chowder with fingerling potatoes and thyme, served with
grilled bread. **Contains**: shellfish, dairy, gluten.

### House Charcuterie Board — $22
Rotating selection of three cured meats, one local cheese, pickled vegetables,
mustard, and crostini. **Contains**: gluten, dairy; specific meats/cheese vary by
week — ask your server for the current selection.
